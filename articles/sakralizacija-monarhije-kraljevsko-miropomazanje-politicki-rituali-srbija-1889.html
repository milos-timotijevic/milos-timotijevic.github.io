Апстракт

Рад анализира обележавање петстоте годишњице Косовске битке и миропомазање краља Александра Обреновића 1889. године као јединствен политички ритуал усмерен на производњу легитимитета у условима династичке и институционалне кризе. Истраживање показује да су свечаности у Крушевцу и Жичи, путовање владара кроз централну Србију и присуство руског посланика били део пажљиво обликованог система јавне репрезентације власти. Анализа се заснива на штампи, службеним програмима и мемоарској грађи, уз тумачење симболичких елемената церемонијала. Иако је ритуал привремено учврстио ауторитет младог владара, није могао да отклони структурне политичке сукобе, чиме се открива и ограничен домет симболичке политике.

Abstract

This article examines the anointing of the young King Alexander Obrenović in 1889 as part of a broader system of public ceremonies and symbolic practices in late nineteenth-century Serbia. The ritual was not an isolated religious act, but an integral component of a carefully designed state and dynastic program, which included the celebration of the five-hundredth anniversary of the Battle of Kosovo, festivities in Kruševac, the anointing ceremony at the monastery of Žiča, and the royal journey through central Serbia.

The article is based on the assumption that monarchical order does not rest solely on institutions, but also on the public representation and perception of power. In this context, ceremonies and symbolic acts contributed to the formation of royal authority by making it visible and socially acceptable. This process unfolded during a period of profound political crisis, when the prestige and stability of the Obrenović dynasty had been seriously undermined.

The festivities in Kruševac demonstrate how the Kosovo tradition was incorporated into contemporary political reality. Through rituals, public performances, and symbolic objects, the past was activated as an instrument of political action, while the young king was presented as the heir to the Kosovo narrative of liberation and unification. Such use of tradition served a clear purpose—to compensate for the weakened authority of the dynasty in the present—while underlying social divisions and political tensions remained unresolved.

The journey to Žiča functioned as a gradual introduction to the culminating act of ritual confirmation of power, while the presence of the Russian envoy pointed to its international dimension. The anointing ceremony itself is interpreted as a renewal of the medieval Nemanjić tradition of the Serbian kingdom, thereby linking the authority of the young ruler to the highest historical model of Serbian statehood. This continuity had a distinctly political function, serving to reinforce legitimacy under unstable conditions.

After the anointing, the king’s journey through central Serbia acquired a distinct function. Visits to sites associated with insurgent and dynastic history represented a ritual incorporation of the young ruler into the tradition of the Obrenović dynasty and an attempt to further consolidate his authority.

The article demonstrates that such ceremonies could produce a powerful impression of unity and stability, but were unable to resolve the underlying contradictions and institutional weaknesses of the political order. The anointing of 1889 is therefore interpreted as a moment that most clearly reveals the attempt to restore the shaken prestige of the dynasty through symbolic forms of public action.

Изворна грађа / Source Material

Основу истраживања чине оновремена штампа (између осталог Српске новине, Браник и Застава), службени програм свечаности „Ред светковине косовске петстогодишњице”, мемоарска грађа и дневници. Штампа је истовремено извор за реконструкцију церемонија и предмет анализе њихове јавне интерпретације.

The study draws on contemporary newspapers (including Srpske novine, Branik and Zastava), the official programme of festivities, memoirs and diaries. The press serves both as evidence for reconstructing the ceremonies and as an object of analysis of their public interpretation.

Изворни корпус обухвата и описе јавних декорација, говора, обреда, одликовања, војних почасти и владарске маршруте. Ова приступна страна не представља посебан дигитализовани корпус извора.

The source corpus also includes descriptions of decorations, speeches, rituals, awards, military honours and the royal itinerary. This landing page does not constitute a separate digitised source corpus.
